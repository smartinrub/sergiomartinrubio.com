## Zalaryx

**Zalaryx** helps you work out what you actually take home — not just what's written on your contract. It compares salaries, freelance rates, taxes and employer costs across **9 countries**: the United Kingdom, the United States, Germany, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Italy, Portugal and Bulgaria.

Whether you are evaluating a job offer, relocating abroad or switching to freelance work, Zalaryx shows your real net income instantly.

> A €80,000 salary in Berlin is not the same as €80,000 in Lisbon, and a £500/day contract rate looks very different after tax.

### Features

- Salary calculator for both employees and freelancers.
- Gross-to-net income breakdown, with a visual split across taxes, contributions and take-home pay.
- Income tax, social security, pension and employer cost calculations using country specific rules.
- Freelancer tax buffer recommendations, so you know how much to set aside.
- Compare compensation across countries and employment types.
- Current tax year calculations for accurate planning.
- Save and compare multiple calculations.

### Who it is for

- Remote workers and digital nomads choosing where to live.
- Freelancers and contractors planning rates and taxes.
- Employees comparing international job offers.
- Professionals considering self-employment.
- Recruiters and HR teams explaining compensation packages.

### Technologies

Built as a native **iOS** application with **Swift**.
