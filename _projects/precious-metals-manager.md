## Precious Metals Manager

**Precious Metals Manager** is an iOS app for managing your precious metals portfolio. Keeping track of your gold, silver, platinum and palladium holdings is complicated, so the app makes it simple: an intuitive interface plus all the metrics and statistics that keep you on top of your assets.

### Features

- Live value and average cost tracking.
- Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) analytics.
- Performance comparison against Bitcoin and other assets.
- Detailed inventory management for bullion.
- Local device storage, with optional iCloud sync.
- Face ID and Touch ID protection.
- Tax reports and CSV export (Pro).
- Price and ratio alerts (Pro).
- Universal app for both iPhone and iPad.

### Technologies

Built as a native **iOS** and **iPadOS** application with **Swift**.
